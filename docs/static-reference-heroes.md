# Static LiFei reference heroes

The homepage uses the three supplied 851 × 1848 reference artworks, including their original product photography, decorative elements, logo, icons, typography, and buttons. Assets in `public/hero` are lossless WebP conversions; the product-header assets are unaltered crops of the top 146 pixels. No artwork was generated or redesigned.

The red hero is present in the initial HTML and preloaded before the application starts. All three unchanged artworks form a horizontal carousel. Only the active slide is interactive and exposed to assistive technology; off-screen images use native lazy loading. Each hero fills the full width and stable viewport height, including the overlapping header. The sharp artwork is centered and scaled proportionally to cover the viewport, cropping excess artwork instead of stretching it or introducing blurred side panels. Desktop, tablet, and mobile landscape screens intentionally crop more of the original portrait composition, as requested. The initial HTML uses the same cover sizing so application loading does not change the image scale.

On tall portrait screens, transparent shopping controls scale with the artwork and remain aligned with its original buttons. At viewport aspect ratios of 1:2 or wider, those controls are hidden and visible shopping links are placed above the slide indicators, keeping them usable when the original buttons are cropped. Safe-area spacing and wrapping protect these links on tablets and landscape phones. Both shopping links use the corresponding real product route on the current domain. Loaded Shopify handles take precedence over the verified initial handles. Search, account, bag, and menu controls use the existing store callbacks. Previous and next arrows wrap in both directions and overlay the left and right edges of the filled hero on every screen size. Three indicators overlay the bottom-right of the hero to select the corresponding slide. The overlay passes pointer events through to the artwork except on its controls. All controls have accessible labels and 44-pixel touch targets. Focus the carousel or its controls to use Left/Right, Home, and End. Horizontal touch or pen swipes navigate; vertical scrolling and pinch zoom remain native. Gestures beginning on links or controls are excluded to avoid accidental purchases or slide changes. No timers, autoplay, or animated transitions are used.

The existing header is sticky at the top of the page, above the carousel and other content but below modal overlays. Its frame spans the full available width, with safe-area padding protecting controls rather than limiting the header to a narrow central column. On phones, its original header crop supplies the unchanged logo, tagline, and icons. At widths of 768 pixels and above, the existing brand mark and tagline sit on the left, with the same search, account, bag, and menu actions on the right. Tablet and desktop header heights remain between 76 and 96 pixels, rather than enlarging the portrait artwork's header to desktop dimensions. After scrolling more than eight pixels, the primary page header becomes solid white with readable dark action icons. Returning to the top restores the active hero's original colored header and white branding. Dimensions remain constant during scrolling to prevent layout jumps. The homepage artwork extends underneath the sticky header. A ResizeObserver, with resize and orientation listeners, updates the actual primary header height for anchor spacing and the mobile menu's available height. Menus open below the sticky header, scroll independently when necessary, and close with Escape. The header in existing product-detail views remains part of their non-scrolling header region.

The three Shopify product IDs in `src/data/referenceHeroes.ts` restrict the matching brand headers to their corresponding product details. Other products retain their previous detail header. Existing prices, variants, images, purchase controls, Shopify integration, and checkout logic are unchanged. The floating quick-add widget remains available after the homepage hero leaves the viewport, rather than covering the reference artwork.

## Verification

Run `npm run lint`, `npm run test:storefront`, and `npm run test:shopify` for type checking and regression tests. The platform performs the production build and deployment separately.

For visual comparison, capture each slide and compare the visible artwork with the corresponding file in `public/hero`. Confirm that sharp artwork fills the viewport edge to edge without blurred side panels and that both arrows overlay the hero on phones and desktops. Cropping is expected; distortion is not. Confirm that scrolling changes the sticky header to solid white with readable controls and returning to the top restores the original colored artwork without a height change.

Also check 320 × 568, 390 × 844, 430 × 932, their landscape equivalents, 768 × 1024, 1024 × 768, 1440 × 900, and large desktop viewports. Confirm that artwork retains its aspect ratio, header controls align, links remain accessible, indicators and arrows work and wrap, inactive slides cannot receive focus, and there is no horizontal overflow. Scroll to the bottom and back quickly; the header must stay at the top with the same dimensions. Open and scroll the mobile menu, resize or rotate the viewport, and check keyboard focus and reduced motion. Wait longer than four seconds and move the pointer or scroll; the selected hero must not change. Real iOS Safari and Android devices should also be checked for native touch and momentum scrolling.

After platform deployment, repeat these checks on `lifeibeauty.com`, including both shopping links, homepage logo, search, account, menu, bag, browser-back navigation, the three corresponding product headers, and an unrelated product page. Local emulation is not a substitute for final production and physical-device verification.
